# Privacy Policy — Zen Blocks Sudoku

**Effective date:** July 26, 2026
**Last updated:** July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zen Blocks Sudoku (the "Game") handles information. Please read it before using the Game.

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data ourselves.** The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers of ours that receive information about you.

The Game does show advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and it offers a one-time purchase that removes advertising. To do those two things the Game includes Google software that connects to Google's servers and, for advertising, uses an advertising identifier from your device. That processing is described below and is carried out by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

If you buy the ad-free unlock, the Game stops requesting ads altogether.

## Information We Collect

We collect nothing. We receive no personal data about you, and we operate no servers.

Specifically, the Game does **not** ask you for or send us:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact details
- Account or profile information (the Game has no accounts)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or file contents
- Usage analytics or crash telemetry

What Google collects for advertising and for purchases is described under **Advertising** and **Purchases** below. We see only aggregate, non-identifying reporting from Google — for example how many ads were shown and what they earned — never information about an individual player.

## Advertising

The Game shows two kinds of ads through **Google AdMob**:

- a banner at the top of the screen while you play, and
- an optional video ad you can choose to watch at the end of a game to carry that game on. It is always your choice; nothing in the Game requires you to watch an ad.

To serve those ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which is included in the Game, sends information to Google. This typically includes your device's advertising identifier, general device and network information, your IP address, and whether you saw or interacted with an ad. Google uses this to select and measure ads, and processes it as an independent data controller under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Details of the data Google collects in this role are published in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game shows you Google's consent form before requesting any ads, and requests ads according to the choices you make there. You can change those choices at any time: open the settings (the gear icon, or Settings in the main menu) and choose **Privacy options**.

**Turning ads off entirely.** The ad-free unlock described below stops the Game from requesting ads at all, on that device and any device signed in to the same Google Play account.

## Purchases

The Game offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase that permanently removes advertising. There is nothing else to buy, and there are no subscriptions.

Purchases are processed by **Google Play** using the Google Play Billing Library, which is included in the Game. Google Play handles payment entirely: **we never see or receive your payment details, billing address, or Google account.** What the Game receives back is whether the unlock is owned, and it keeps a copy of that answer on your device so it can start ad-free without a network connection. Google Play holds the authoritative record, which is what restores the unlock if you reinstall the Game or change device.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policies.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The Game saves a small amount of data locally so it can work as expected — for example:

- Your unfinished game: the board, the pieces still waiting to be placed, the score so far, the mode it is being played in, and whether the game has already been carried on once
- Your best score
- Your settings and preferences (sound, vibration, the game mode you picked and the options you set for it, and similar)
- Whether the ad-free unlock has been bought

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us and it is not accessible to us. Deleting or uninstalling the Game removes it, and clearing the Game's data through your device settings resets it. Google's own software stores its own data on your device as part of serving ads and processing purchases; that data is Google's and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

## Permissions

The Game itself asks only for **vibration**, for the small taps you feel when placing a piece. It can be turned off in the settings.

The Google software included in the Game adds the permissions it needs to do its work: internet and network access, so ads and purchases can reach Google's servers; Google Play billing; and access to your device's advertising identifier for advertising. Android may list these against the Game because they ship inside it.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game shows advertising supplied by Google, it is intended for a general audience of 13 and over. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that concerns you, contact us at the address below.

## Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to its sale or sharing.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or port. For the advertising data Google processes, you can:

- change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time through **Privacy options** in the Game's settings;
- reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Google settings;
- remove advertising from the Game entirely by buying the ad-free unlock;
- exercise your rights directly with Google, as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Data saved on your device remains fully under your control.

## Security

We operate no servers, so there is no server-side data of ours that could be exposed in a breach. Information sent to Google for advertising and purchases travels over connections secured by Google's software. Locally stored game data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the Game changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted in the Game's release notes. Continued use of the Game after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
