# Privacy Policy — Zen Blocks Sudoku

**Effective date:** July 25, 2026
**Last updated:** July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zen Blocks Sudoku (the "Game") handles information. Please read it before using the Game.

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

The Game runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers that receive information about you.

## Information We Collect

None.

Specifically, the Game does **not** collect:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact details
- Account or profile information (the Game has no accounts)
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or file contents
- Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioral tracking data

## Data Stored on Your Device

The Game saves a small amount of data locally so it can work as expected — for example:

- Your unfinished game: the board, the pieces still waiting to be placed, and the score so far
- Your best score
- Your settings and preferences (sound, vibration, how many pieces are offered, and similar)

This data never leaves your device. It is not accessible to us. Deleting or uninstalling the Game removes it, and clearing the Game's data through your device settings resets it.

## Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs.

If you obtained the Game through an app store or distribution platform, that platform may collect information about your download and purchases under its own privacy policy. We do not control and do not receive that information. Please refer to the privacy policy of the platform you used.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because no data is collected, there is nothing to disclose, delete, or request access to.

## Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to its sale or sharing. Because we do not collect or process any personal data, and do not sell or share any data, there is no personal information for us to act on. Data saved on your device remains fully under your control.

## Security

Since no data is transmitted or stored on our systems, there is no server-side data that could be exposed in a breach. Locally stored game data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the Game changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted in the Game's release notes. Continued use of the Game after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
